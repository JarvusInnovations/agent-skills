# Plain language word list

Quick-lookup substitutions. The tables under "From the guide" reproduce the examples on
digital.gov's Plain Language Guide (https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language). The last
section adds common substitutions in the same spirit that the guide does not list; treat
those as conventions, not as part of the framework.

Prefer the left column. Keep the right column only when it is the exact term the audience
uses and expects.

## From the guide

### Familiar words instead of formal ones

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| you | addressee |
| aid, help | assist, assistance |
| begin, start | commence |
| carry out, start | implement |
| for, so | in order that |
| for | in the amount of |
| if | in the event of |
| issue, publish | promulgate |
| us, we | this activity, command |
| use | utilize, utilization |

### Prepositional phrases that hide a short word

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| several, a few, many | a number of |
| enough | a sufficient number of |
| now | at this point in time |
| can | is able to |
| monthly | on a monthly basis |
| because | on the ground that |
| X | an amount of X |
| must | be responsible for |
| to | in order to |

### Hidden verbs (nominalizations)

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| We analyze data. | We conduct an analysis of the data. |
| We manage the program. | We are responsible for management of the program. |

Hidden verbs end in -ment, -tion, -sion, -ance, or -ing, often sit between "the" and "of",
and often need an empty verb such as achieve, effect, give, have, make, reach, or take.

### Doublets and triplets

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| due | due and payable |
| stop | cease and desist |
| either knowledge or information | knowledge and information |

### Empty modifiers

absolutely, actually, completely, really, quite, totally, very

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| Their claim was absurd. | Their claim was totally unrealistic. |
| HUD and FAA issued a report. | HUD and FAA issued a joint report. |
| This information is critical. | This information is really critical. |

### Requirements, permissions, recommendations

| Say | Meaning |
|---|---|
| must | obligation |
| must not | prohibition |
| may | discretionary action |
| should | recommendation |

Do not use "shall". It is ambiguous between obligation and prediction and rarely appears
in everyday speech.

### Double negatives

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| at least | no fewer than |
| is under | has not yet attained |
| may only ... when | may not ... until |
| only if | is not ... unless |
| You must get the agency's express approval for any noise compatibility program. | No approval of any noise compatibility program may be implied in the absence of the agency's express approval. |

Ordinary words with a negative meaning: unless, fail to, notwithstanding, except, other than.
Watch for them after "not" and find a positive word instead.

### Abbreviations

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| the committee | ESAC (Engineering Safety Advisory Committee) |
| waste handlers | SQHUW (Small-quantity handlers of universal wastes) |
| the Act | FPERA (Fire and Police Employee Relations Act) |

- Use an abbreviation without definition only if everyone knows it: IBM, ATM, PhD, CIA, FBI.
- If you must abbreviate, define it at first use and keep to two or three per document.
- Write "for example" or "such as", not "e.g.". Write "that is", not "i.e.".

### Noun strings

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| Developing procedures to protect the safety of workers in underground mines | Underground mine worker safety protection procedures development |
| Draft regulations to protect the rights of laboratory animals | Draft laboratory animal rights protection regulations |

### Slashes

Decide what you mean. "And/or" becomes "or", "and", or "either X, or Y, or both". Use a
hyphen for paired equal terms: "faculty-student ratio".

## Additions beyond the guide

Common substitutions in the same spirit. Not part of the digital.gov framework.

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| try | endeavor, attempt |
| end | terminate |
| about | regarding, concerning, with respect to, in regard to |
| buy | purchase |
| need | require (when you mean need) |
| before | prior to |
| after | subsequent to |
| because | due to the fact that |
| for | for the purpose of |
| each | each and every |
| void | null and void |
| we decide | we make a decision |
| we consider | we give consideration to |
| we review | we perform a review of |
